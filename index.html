Scope

The proposed project focuses on the design and development of a low-cost cyclone dust separator to improve air quality in small and medium-scale industrial workshops in Pakistan. Scope

The existing baffle arrangement will first be studied to establish baseline thermal and hydraulic performance. Scope

**Scope** This project covers the design, fabrication and experimental characterisation of a jet thermocompressor for flash steam recovery. The work includes thermodynamic sizing of the motive nozzle, mixing chamber and diffuser for a specified duty, followed by CAD modelling, manufacturing drawings, and fabrication of the assembly with interchangeable nozzle inserts. A test rig will be built and instrumented for pressure, temperature and flow on the motive, suction and discharge streams. Testing will be conducted on compressed air as a safe analogue to steam, with results extrapolated analytically to steam conditions. Entrainment ratio and compression ratio will be measured against nozzle area ratio, throat position and diffuser angle to identify the geometry giving best performance. Deliverables are a working thermocompressor, measured performance curves, a validated design methodology, and an assessment of achievable energy savings. Live steam testing, pressure vessel certification and full-scale plant installation fall outside this scope.

Streams

Thermofluids Mechanical Design

Deliverables

1A fabricated jet thermocompressor assembly with interchangeable motive nozzle inserts, and an instrumented test rig measuring pressure, temperature and flow on the motive, suction and discharge streams.
2Measured performance curves giving entrainment ratio and compression ratio as functions of nozzle area ratio, throat position, diffuser angle and motive pressure.
3A validated design and sizing methodology relating required duty — suction pressure, discharge pressure and flow — to nozzle and diffuser geometry, verified against the experimental data.
4A complete engineering package: CAD models, manufacturing drawings, bill of materials with local supplier costing, and a test procedure.
5A techno-economic assessment of flash steam recovery for a representative Pakistani industrial steam system, giving projected fuel savings, capital cost and payback period.
